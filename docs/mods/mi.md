### High Pressure Loop: Advanced Boiler <-> Heat Exchanger

![High Pressure Loop](img/MI HP loop.png#only-light)
![High Pressure Loop](img/MI HP loop.dk.png#only-dark)

A pressurizer is need to initially prime the loop with HP water.
It won't take much, as 125mB of HP water takes up _16 times_ as much volume when turned to HP steam (2,000mB).
 
If you are running (and thus boiling) HP water or HP heavy-water in a fission reactor, a small portion will be converted to Deuterium or Tritium, and this will amount will need to be continually replaced.
